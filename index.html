    Underground operating rules used in this trainer

    • Horn: a working dash button. Site horn-signal rules vary, so they are not scored in this trainer – the focus is on the controls.
    • Lights on whenever the machine is moving. Headlights and reflective delineators are your only reference underground.
    • Speed limit 15 km/h in drives, 10 km/h loaded near draw points and the ore pass.
    • Travel height: bucket racked back, 0.3 – 0.8 m off the floor. Never tram with the boom raised – it destroys stability and your view.
    • Stop fully before changing direction. Shuttle-shifting damages the transmission.
    • Park brake on whenever stopped for any length of time. Steering & transmission lock lever ON at start-up and before leaving the seat.
    • Loading: bucket flat on the floor, approach the muckpile square and slowly, crowd in with a steady accelerator and rack back in short bursts while lifting slightly. Do not spin the tyres.
    • Dumping: stop at the dump marker, raise the bucket clear of the bund, creep forward until the tyres touch the bund, dump, rack back, reverse away and lower to travel height.
    • Never drive into the draw point brow or under unsupported ground. Stay clear of the ore pass edge.
